# Scan2Chat Project Guide

This file explains what the app is for, what it can do, and the important things to know. It is written in simple language with no technical terms.

---

## 1. What is the app? (Main Goal)

Scan2Chat is a mobile app that lets you scan visiting cards (business cards) using your phone's camera. It automatically reads the name, phone number, and email written on the card. Then you can instantly start a WhatsApp chat or save the contact to your phone's address book, without typing anything by hand.

---

## 2. What can you do with the app? (Main Features)

- **Scan visiting cards** – Point your phone camera at a card. The app will automatically pick up the name, phone numbers, and email.
- **Start WhatsApp chat** – Open a WhatsApp conversation with a scanned number in just one tap.
- **Save to phone contacts** – Save one contact or all scanned contacts to your phone's contact list with one tap.
- **Export contacts** – Download all scanned contacts as a file (CSV) and share it with anyone.
- **View past scans** – All scanned contacts stay in the app, where you can edit or delete them.
- **Create an account and log in** – Make an account so your saved scans are kept safe.
- **Updates and notifications** – Get news about new app versions and messages from the Scan2Chat team.

---

## 3. Important things to know

- **App name:** Scan2Chat
- **Made by:** Proximatix
- **Version:** 3.1.0 (build 8)
- **Where it runs:** Mobile phones, and it can also run on computers and the web.
- **Admin panel:** There is a separate website page where the admin can manage users, scan history, and notices.
- **Scan backup:** When you have internet, your scanned contacts are saved online so they are not lost if you change your phone.
- **Card photo:** When scanning, a photo of the card can also be taken and saved online.
- **Language:** The app is in English.
